Otium · icon refinement, round 2

Five variations each of Orbit and Dusk. Under each: the icon at 32 and 16px, then the one-colour menu bar version on light and dark.

Orbit The Glance view's ring as the brand mark.

O1 · Classic

Round 1 as it was: bold ring, centre dot.

O2 · Thin

Lighter strokes, a longer arc. More refined, less sturdy at 16px.

O3 · Progress head

A dot leads the arc like a clock hand's tip. Feels like time moving.

O4 · Stone

Classic ring, sage on warm travertine. Calmer, stands out from green apps.

O5 · Orbit × Dusk

Hybrid: the day's ring with the sun setting inside it.

Dusk The sun at the horizon: time to step away.

D1 · Classic

Round 1 as it was: sun, horizon, two reflection lines.

D2 · Bold

Bigger sun, one line. The simplest; strongest at 16px.

D3 · Window

The scene framed in a circle, like looking out a window.

D4 · Glow

Warm dusk light: sage fading to apricot, a soft halo.

D5 · Rays

A gentle sunburst: more uplifting, a little busier.
