2 · Beyond colour

Options that change the feel more than the palette does.

High contrast

Accessibility

Stronger text, borders and rings. Could follow macOS “Increase contrast”.

Glass

Native macOS feel

A frosted, translucent panel that lets the desktop colours show through, like Control Center. (Simulated here over a sample wallpaper.)

Serif typeface

Editorial, Roman

The same panel set in a serif (Apple’s New York, falling back to Iowan Old Style). Fits the Latin name. A rounded face was explored too but would need a bundled font.

3 · Follow the day

An optional mode unique to Otium: the theme shifts with the time of day, echoing the app icon.

→ →

The panel changes gently over the day, so a glance also tells you what part of the day it is, and the evening palette is warmer and dimmer as you wind down.

Changes preview instantly.

  • Theme swatches: each circle shows its light and dark background, with the accent dot. Clicking one previews it immediately.
  • Follow day sits with the swatches, as a theme of its own.
  • Mode: System follows macOS; Light and Dark override it just for Otium.
  • Glass, Typeface, High contrast work on top of any theme.
  • The menu bar icon, notifications and app icon stay as macOS draws them.
