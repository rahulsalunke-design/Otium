Otium · List view prototype

B4, D4 and a Hybrid of the two share the same live timers, like the real app would. Hover rows, pause, reset and snooze; speed time up to watch rings and bars move, or make a reminder go off.

B4Expand on hover

Otium
  • Hover (or Tab to) a row: it expands to show the interval, next time and Reset.
  • Pause / Play is always one click away.
  • When a reminder goes off the row glows and a Snooze pill appears.

D4Big time

Otium
  • The ring empties as time passes. Hover the ring: the icon turns into Reset; click to restart.
  • The large countdown and Pause / Play stay visible on every row.
  • When a reminder goes off: the row glows and "Snooze 5 min" replaces the next time.

HHybrid · B4 × D4

Otium
  • Compact by default: a small ring gauge, name, countdown and Pause / Play.
  • Hover (or Tab to) a row: it expands to show how often it repeats (e.g. "every 60 min"), plus a Snooze button.
  • Hover the ring: it turns into Reset; click to restart.
  • When a reminder goes off the row glows and Snooze stays visible without hovering.
